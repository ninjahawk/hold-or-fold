| ImpossibleBench (Zhong, Raghunathan, Carlini et al., 2025-10) | 2510.20270 | "Cheating rate": pass rate on task variants whose unit tests conflict with the spec (LiveCodeBench, SWE-bench), i.e. test exploitation | It measures what agents *do* to the tests; this project measures what agents *say* about their own finished work (stated confidence vs hidden tests) and whether they defend or abandon it under rebuttal. | ForecastBench (Karger et al., 2024-09) | 2409.19839 | Accuracy (and calibration) of forecasts on 1,000 auto-generated future-event questions vs human forecasters | Calibration about the world; ours is calibration about the model's own just-produced artifact, scored minutes later by execution. |
| Why Language Models Hallucinate (Kalai et al., 2025-09) | 2509.04664 | Argument that training/evaluation reward guessing over admitting uncertainty | Theory/motivation; we give an empirical measure of overclaiming in agentic coding. |
| FlipFlop / "Are You Sure?" (Laban et al., 2023-11) | 2311.08596 | Ten LLMs on seven classification tasks: "Are you sure?" makes models flip 46% of the time, accuracy drops 17% | Classification, one neutral challenge; we use code with hidden-test truth, three escalating rebuttals (neutral re-check / reviewer says it fails / tests fail), and split flips into warranted (wrong to right) vs capitulation (right to wrong). |
| **closest** Thinking-in-Bets (Bui, 2026-10) | 2610.34654 | Agents abandoning correct work when challenged (false reversals) vs ignoring real mistakes; method: write a claim checklist before reading feedback; five Gemini models, SWE and other benchmarks | Overlaps strongly on the defense half (false reversals of correct work, real-mistake fixing). It is a mitigation paper on Gemini models; ours is a measurement on Claude Haiku/Sonnet and an open 7B model, joins defense with calibration of the stated confidence, and uses trap variants to get confident-wrong cases. Novelty for the defense curve alone is NOT safe: frame the contribution as the calibration-defense link (does stated confidence predict who holds when right and folds when wrong). |
| **closest** Done Is Not Correct (Rodrigues et al., 2026-11) | 2611.10063 | Silent failures and completion-claim calibration of LLM agents writing CAD code; 13 tasks, 4 models; measure-then-claim loop cuts silent failures | Overlaps on the calibration half (completion claim vs checked truth). | The Confidence Dichotomy (Xuan et al., 2026-01) | 2601.07264 | Verbalized calibration in tool-use agents: evidence tools (search) cause overconfidence, verification tools (code interpreter) reduce it; RL fix | Task-answer calibration; ours targets claims about the agent's own code, and the rebuttal phase.
